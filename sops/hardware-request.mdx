Standardize how people request, receive, and track hardware (MacBooks, monitors, peripherals, replacements).

**Tools:** Assist (Slack → Jira), asset inventory, Jamf.

## Procedure

1. **Request** — Staff opens `/assist` in Slack. Include hardware type, why it is needed, and a shipping address if they are remote.
2. **Approval** — Second monitors, standing desks, and similar extras may need manager or Finance approval.
3. **Fulfillment** — IT checks inventory, assigns the device, installs Jamf / required software, and updates the asset tracker (user, serial, date issued).
4. **Delivery** — Office pickup, or ship with tracking for remote staff.

## Tips

- Update inventory whenever a device is reassigned.
- Use `/assist` for every request so the trail stays auditable.
- Confirm the power adapter is included.
