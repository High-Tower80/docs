Protect accounts with a password manager and phishing-resistant MFA. The policy behind this SOP: [Identity and Entra](/policies/identity-entra).

**Tools:** Entra ID (branded Microsoft login), Google Workspace, 1Password or Bitwarden, Microsoft Authenticator or a passkey.

## First login and MFA

1. You should see the **branded Microsoft login** with the Intertrend logo. If you do not, stop and tell IT. That is how you avoid a look-alike page.
2. Enter your Intertrend email → **Next**.
3. Enroll **Microsoft Authenticator with number matching**, or a passkey / security key if IT issued one.
4. Do **not** pick SMS or voice as your only method. If the wizard pushes text messages, finish Authenticator first and tell IT so we can strip SMS.

Lost device: `/assist`. IT resets MFA after an identity check. Do not ask a coworker to approve your prompt.

## Password rules

- Long passphrase or a manager-generated secret. 12 characters is the floor, not a goal.
- No reuse. No names, seasons, or `Intertrend2026!`.
- Store secrets in the password manager. Never in Docs, Sheets, Slack, or a sticky note.
- Rotate when IT says so, or immediately if a breach is suspected. Calendar rotation every 12 months is not a substitute for a manager and MFA.

## Non-negotiables

- Never approve an MFA prompt you did not start. Decline it and tell IT.
- Keep a second approved method (second device or passkey), not a second SMS number.
- Shared mailboxes and service accounts are IT-owned. Do not invent a shared password in a channel.

June 2026: a real mailbox was used to send unauthorized mail after SMS-class MFA was still in the mix. We are not going back to "text me a code" as the default.
