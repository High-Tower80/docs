Protect accounts with strong passwords and MFA through Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD).

**Tools:** Google Workspace, Entra ID, a password manager (1Password or Bitwarden), and an authenticator app.

## First login and MFA

1. You should see the **branded Microsoft login** with the Intertrend logo and background. If you do not, stop and tell IT.
2. Enter your Intertrend email → **Next**.
3. Choose phone call, text, or authenticator app, then finish the prompts.

## Password rules

- At least 12 characters, mixed case, numbers, and symbols
- No dictionary words, personal names, or reused passwords
- Store secrets in a password manager — never in Docs, Sheets, or sticky notes
- Rotate at least every 12 months, or immediately if a breach is suspected

Use `/assist` if you need a reset. Lost device: contact IT so MFA can be reset after identity check.

## Tips

- Never approve an MFA prompt you did not start.
- Keep a backup method on file.
