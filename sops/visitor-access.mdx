How to host guests, vendors, and contractors on site without leaving the office open.

**Tools:** visitor sign-in (digital or paper), temp badges, Slack or calendar for notice.

## Procedure

1. **Register** — Add the visitor to the shared calendar or visitor list: host, guest name, date/time, purpose.
2. **Check-in** — Guests sign in and show ID if asked. The host greets them promptly.
3. **Badge** — Issue a temp badge or label if you use them. Guests wear it where it can be seen.
4. **Escort** — Stay with visitors in secure or restricted areas. The host owns guest conduct.
5. **Check-out** — Sign out and return the badge.

## Tips

- Tell the front desk or Facilities about large groups or VIPs ahead of time.
- Multi-day contractors may need a recurring pass.
- Keep the daily visitor log.
