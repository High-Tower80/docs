Respond to phishing, mailbox takeover, data leaks, and outages. Everyone can report. IT owns the first hour. KMicro is the SOC / IR partner for identity and mail.

The approved plan: [Incident response](/policies/incident-response). Staff reporting: [Phishing alert guide](/how-to/phishing-alert-guide). IT playbook: [Phishing triage](/sops/phishing-triage).

**Tools:** Entra / Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Slack Assist, Jamf Protect, admin audit logs, KMicro.

## Procedure

1. **Detect and report** — `/assist` in Slack. Escalate immediately if mail is sending itself or MFA prompts appear unprompted.
2. **Triage** — Low / Medium / High / Critical. Isolate devices. For mailbox or Entra: revoke sessions, kill inbox rules and forwarding, strip MFA, check OAuth apps. Do not "reset the password and hope."
3. **Escalate** — Identity or unauthorized outbound mail → KMicro the same day. Do not wait to write a perfect summary.
4. **Respond** — Revoke Google, Slack, SaaS as needed. Scan or reimage. Notify people who were impersonated or mailed.
5. **Document** — Private Drive folder. Timeline, systems, actions. No client names in public Slack.
6. **Review** — Postmortem with gaps. Update the policy if the plan was wrong.

## Tips

- Report phishing even when you are unsure.
- Escalate early. Keep chat and audit logs.
- If Entra logging is still thin, say that in the write-up. Do not invent visibility.
