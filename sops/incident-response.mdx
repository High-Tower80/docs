Respond to phishing, data leaks, and outages. Everyone can report; IT and Security own the response.

**Tools:** Google Workspace, Slack Assist, admin audit logs, Jamf.

## Procedure

1. **Detect and report** — `/assist` or [help@intertrend.atlassian.net](mailto:help@intertrend.atlassian.net). Escalate critical issues immediately.
2. **Triage** — Severity Low / Medium / High / Critical. Isolate affected devices or accounts.
3. **Respond** — Revoke Google, Slack, and SaaS access as needed. Scan or reimage devices. Notify impacted people.
4. **Document** — Internal report with timeline, systems, and actions. Store it securely in Drive.
5. **Review** — Postmortem. Update SOPs, tooling, or training.

Staff-facing reporting steps: [Phishing alert guide](/how-to/phishing-alert-guide). IT playbook: [Phishing triage](/sops/phishing-triage).

## Tips

- Report phishing even when you are unsure.
- Escalate early. Keep chat and audit logs.
