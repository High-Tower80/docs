Keep Slack readable and searchable.

## Naming

- `#team-` for departments (`#team-it`)
- `#project-` for time-boxed work (`#project-website`)
- `#help-` for internal requests (`#help-it`)

## Usage

- Use threads.
- Pin important messages or docs.
- Keep the channel topic and description current.
- IT may archive channels idle for 60\+ days.

## External people

Guests belong only in designated shared channels. Announce it when you invite someone from outside Intertrend.

## Tips

- Put requests in the right `#help-` channel instead of a DM so others can find the answer later.

**Contact:** `/assist` in Slack.
