Evacuation and safety for California offices. Applies to everyone on site, including visitors.

**Tools:** exit maps, PA/intercom, evacuation checklist, first-aid kits, Safety Captains (CPR-certified, vests).

## Drills

- Twice a year. Attendance is required unless approved in advance.
- Safety Captains walk teams to the posted assembly area.

## If it is real

1. Follow exit signs. Leave belongings. Stay calm.
2. Meet at the outdoor assembly point posted on your floor.
3. **Do not use elevators.** Keep exits and halls clear.

## What this covers

- Fire or smoke
- Earthquake: Drop, Cover, Hold, then leave if it is safe
- Active threat: Run–Hide–Fight
- Power outage or flooding

Need help evacuating? Tell Facilities **before** a drill or emergency so they can plan for you.
