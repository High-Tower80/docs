Secure removal of access and recovery of company assets. Same-day for involuntary exits. Identity rule: [Identity and Entra](/policies/identity-entra).

**Tools:** Entra ID / Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Slack / Assist, Jamf, Drive and calendar transfer, Brivo, asset tracker, password manager.

## Procedure

1. **Start** — Offboarding ticket via Assist or HR. Exact date and time. Involuntary = do not wait for the goodbye lunch.
2. **Identity** — Revoke Entra / Microsoft 365 sessions and refresh tokens. Disable or block sign-in. Strip MFA methods. Check inbox rules, forwarding, delegates, and OAuth apps on that account.
3. **Google** — Suspend Workspace (do not delete yet). Remove Groups and Drive shares.
4. **Slack and SaaS** — Revoke Slack. Pull Zoom, Notion, password-manager, and other seats.
5. **Data** — Reassign Drive ownership to the manager. Export mail or calendar only if HR / Legal asked.
6. **Hardware** — Collect Mac, charger, accessories. Inspect. Update inventory. Remote staff get a label. Jamf wipe if it is not coming back clean.
7. **Badge** — Facilities kills Brivo.

The old SOP listed Google and Slack and forgot Entra. That is how a "disabled Google user" still sends mail from M365.

## Tips

- Move fast on involuntary exits.
- Reassign shared calendar events.
- Confirm Drive ownership actually moved.
- Confirm the mailbox cannot sign in, not just that it "looks off."
