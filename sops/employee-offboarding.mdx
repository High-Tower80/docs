Secure removal of access and recovery of company assets when someone leaves, including same-day exits.

**Tools:** Google Workspace, Slack / Assist, Jamf, Drive and calendar transfer, Brivo badges, asset tracker.

## Procedure

1. **Start** — Offboarding ticket via Assist or HR. Confirm the exact date and time.
2. **Accounts** — Suspend Google Workspace (do not delete yet). Revoke Slack. Remove Google Groups and Drive shares.
3. **Data** — Reassign Drive ownership to the manager. Export mail, docs, or calendar items if needed.
4. **Hardware** — Collect MacBook, charger, and accessories. Inspect and update inventory. Remote staff get a shipping label.
5. **Badge** — Facilities deactivates door access.
6. **Licenses** — Remove Zoom, Notion, other SaaS, and password-manager seats.

## Tips

- Move quickly on involuntary exits.
- Reassign or remove shared calendar events.
- Confirm Drive ownership actually moved so files are not lost.
