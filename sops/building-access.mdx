How employees get into the building, and what to do with a lost badge.

**Hours on a standard badge:** 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM, Monday–Friday.

## Badge

- New hires get a badge at onboarding or on their first office visit.
- **Lost or stolen:** email [admin@intertrend.com](mailto:admin@intertrend.com) immediately. The old badge is deactivated; a temp or replacement is issued.
- **After hours:** not on by default. Request ahead with `/assist` and manager approval.

## Visitors

Register guests in advance. The host checks them in and escorts them.

## Tips

- Do not lend your badge.
- If it stops working, contact IT or Facilities right away.

**Contact:** [admin@intertrend.com](mailto:admin@intertrend.com) or `/assist`.
