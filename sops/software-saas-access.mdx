Request access to internal tools and third-party SaaS (Zoom, Notion, Figma, 1Password, and similar).

**Tools:** Assist → Jira, Google Groups, license tracker.

## Procedure

1. **Submit** — `/assist` in Slack. Include the tool name, purpose or project, and your manager if approval is required.
2. **IT review** — Confirm a license is free, invite the user or add them to the right Google Group, and check SSO / Google Workspace if the app uses it.
3. **Confirm** — User gets a login link. Access is logged in the internal tracker.

## Tips

- Do not share a teammate’s login. Ask for your own seat.
- Check the [software toolkit](/how-to/software-toolkit) before requesting a brand-new product.
- Requests can wait if the license pool is empty.

**Contact:** [help@intertrend.atlassian.net](mailto:help@intertrend.atlassian.net) or `/assist`.
