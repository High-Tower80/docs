Keep Drive easy to search across teams and clients.

## Folder shape

- `/Clients/ClientName/ProjectName/`
- `/Internal/Department/Docs/`

Prefer Shared drives when the team has them. Grant access with Google Groups, not one-off people, when you can.

## File names

`YYYY-MM-DD_ProjectName_DocumentType`

Example: `2025-03-21_NewWebsite_DesignSpecs.docx`

## Sharing

- Share the folder, not twenty individual files.
- Avoid “Anyone with the link.”
- Give folders an owner and run a quarterly cleanup.

## Tips

- Skip names like `Final_v9_copy_USE_THIS_ONE`.
- Prefer lowercase with hyphens or underscores if people sync locally.
