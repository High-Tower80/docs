Intertrend’s Slack ChatGPT bot works in a **DM** or in any channel. It remembers your last few messages for **15 minutes**.

## Start a chat

1. Search Slack for `@ChatGPT` and open **Messages**. Star it so it stays in your sidebar.
2. In a channel, mention `@ChatGPT` on the same line as your prompt.
3. Type like a normal Slack message and send.

Examples:

- “How can I clear my cache in Chrome?”
- “Write a short blog post on the benefits of remote work.”
- Give it a persona, or follow up so it keeps context (“Plan a trip to Paris” then “What should I see there?”).

## Generate images (DALL·E 3)

Prefix the prompt with `image:`

```text
image: kitty finds its mommy at sunset
image: spiderman in the art style of Jim Lee
```

Images usually take about 8 seconds.

## Chat with a PDF

1. Upload a **text-based** PDF (contracts, invoices, policies work; scanned image PDFs often fail).
2. Wait for the scan.
3. Ask questions. The bot keeps that PDF in context for **about 30 minutes**, or until you upload another.

## Search the Company Wiki

```text
/wiki wifi
```

or type `wiki: pto` in a DM or channel. Results use public wiki links, not raw Notion URLs.

## Good habits

- Be specific. Iterate if the first answer is off.
- Follow the company [AI Policy](https://wiki.intertrendhub.com) for client work and confidential files.
- For bot problems, ping `@Assist` or open a [help ticket](/how-to/submit-a-help-ticket).
