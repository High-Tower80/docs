## Add InterHub to your phone home screen

Treat InterHub like an app:

1. Open [intertrendhub.com](https://intertrendhub.com) in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
2. Use **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** (iOS) or the browser menu → **Add to Home screen** (Android).
3. Keep the InterHub name/icon, then add it.

That gives you one-tap access to timesheets and the rest of the Hub without hunting for a bookmark.
