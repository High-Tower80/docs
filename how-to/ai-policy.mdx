<Note>
  Baseline rules for using AI at Intertrend. This does not replace client contracts, privacy law, or security policy. The 2023 company-wide statement is [Generative AI Use Policy](/policies/generative-ai). When in doubt, do not use AI and contact IT.
</Note>

## Quick checklist

- Approved tool plus a **company** account or company-managed config
- No client confidential info, personal or sensitive data, or credentials
- Data minimized and identifiers redacted
- Facts verified and IP respected
- Outputs stored only in approved systems

## Allowed tools and accounts

Use **IT-approved tools** only, in **company-managed** configurations (SSO or MFA, approved workspaces). Do not use personal accounts for company or client work. Do not bypass privacy or retention controls.

## What not to submit

Minimize data. Prefer synthetic or generalized examples.

<Warning>
  Do not submit client confidential information (unless IT and your manager explicitly approve), personal or sensitive data, credentials, keys, or tokens, non-public financials or contract terms, or anything restricted by an NDA or client or platform terms.
</Warning>

- Redact names, emails, phones, addresses, IDs, and customer IDs
- Prefer tools configured so prompts and outputs are **not used for vendor training**
- Do not paste non-public AI outputs into public tools, forums, or social
- Do not connect unapproved AI plug-ins to email, calendar, Drive, CRM, or ad accounts

**Marketing-specific:** no raw CRM lists, hashed IDs, pixel data, or personal-audience data in AI tools unless explicitly approved. Aggregate or anonymize performance data before analysis.

## Responsible use

You are accountable. AI is a drafting aid.

- Verify facts, performance interpretations, citations, and legal or regulatory statements
- Respect IP and licensing; do not upload client creative assets to unapproved tools
- Avoid bias and harmful targeting; take extra care with minors and sensitive audiences
- Disclose material AI assistance when required by contract, policy, platform rules, or law
- Store outputs only in approved systems
- Report suspected exposure or misuse immediately via [Submit a help ticket](/how-to/submit-a-help-ticket)

Exceptions need written approval from IT and your department manager (and Legal or Privacy when needed). Violations may result in access removal, discipline, and contract remedies.

## Ethics highlights (2026)

Companion to the privacy rules above. Full charter stays in Notion.

- Use AI to amplify human creativity, not replace it
- Do not cut-and-paste AI outputs to clients or external parties. Rewrite in your voice, validate claims, and tailor to the audience
- Supervisor approval is required before using AI to produce videos, images, or copy that will ship as **final** client campaign assets
- No client-facing copy, art, or strategy ships without a final human review (the Human Soul Audit)
- Review AI personas and creative drafts for AAPI stereotyping or cultural flattening. Strategy holds final veto
- If AI significantly shapes campaign creative, assess disclosure requirements for the client and platform
- Do not submit client data or proprietary multicultural insights (including Make Noise Today materials) to open or public AI models
