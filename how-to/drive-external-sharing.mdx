Share Drive files with trusted external partners without using public links.

<Note>
  Share to the partner’s **exact work email**. Do not use “Anyone with the link” or a personal Gmail.
</Note>

## Who can share

Anyone on the team may share what they need for the project. Stay inside that project’s scope.

## Do / don’t

**Do**

- Share to a named address (`jane@partner.com`)
- Pick Viewer, Commenter, or Editor on purpose
- Share a project **folder** so access cascades
- `@` partners in comments only after they already have access

**Don’t**

- “Anyone with the link”
- Personal Gmail as the share target
- Files that are not part of the engagement

## How

1. Right-click the file or folder → **Share**
2. Enter the partner email
3. Set Viewer / Commenter / Editor
4. Send (optional short note)

Prefer one top-level project folder, named like `ClientName - ProjectName`, and put the work inside it.

## If they cannot open it

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| “Request access” | Email spelling and permission level |
| File will not open | Not in Trash; they are on the folder |
| @mention failed | They are not shared on that item yet |

Remove partner access when the work is done. Folder naming details: [Drive naming](/sops/google-drive-naming).
