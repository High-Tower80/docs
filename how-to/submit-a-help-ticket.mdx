<Note>
  Slash commands used in a channel or thread are **not posted to the channel**. They only open the form for you.
</Note>

Open an IT or Facilities ticket from anywhere in Slack.

## Slack

Type any of these commands and send. A form appears just for you:

- `/assist`
- `/halp`
- `/helpdesk`
- `/support`

They all launch the same Assist app. Look for the Assist logo in Slack if you want to check ticket history later. History is shared only with IT.

Do not email a leftover helpdesk address. Slack is the ticket.

## Who answers

- Slack: `@Assist`, `@tate`, `@sam.ellis`

## FAQ

**Will `/assist` notify the channel?** No. Slash commands are not posts.

**Can I track what I submitted?** Yes. Open the Assist app and view your ticket history.
