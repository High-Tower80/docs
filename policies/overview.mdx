## How to read status

- **Required** means the control must exist. Missing = gap, not a suggestion.
- **Current state** is what we can document from Drive, Jamf, or incident write-ups. It is allowed to be ugly.
- **Open gaps** are the work. Do not delete a gap because it is embarrassing.
