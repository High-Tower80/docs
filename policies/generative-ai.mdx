## Purpose

People will use generative AI. The company does not pretend otherwise. The rule is: approved tools, company accounts, no secrets in the prompt, human review before anything ships.

## Scope

Employees, contractors, consultants, and anyone else on Intertrend systems or Intertrend data.

## Rules

- Use an **IT-approved** tool on a **company** account. Personal ChatGPT / Gemini / Claude on a side account is not "fine for a draft" when the draft has client work in it.
- Opt out of vendor training / data sharing where the product allows it.
- **Never** put in financials, PII, trade secrets, passwords, API keys, raw CRM lists, or anything that would let an outsider into Intertrend or a client.
- Give the tool the minimum needed. Redact names.
- Treat output as a draft. Review facts, code, legal claims, and cultural representation. The 2023 PDF worried about bad architecture advice. Still true. The 2026 staff page adds the Human Soul Audit for client creative. Both apply.
- Do not forward raw output outside the company.
- Do not connect unapproved AI plugins to mail, calendar, Drive, or ad accounts.
- Business use on company devices. Do not run a personal AI side project on a company Mac if it needs company data.

IT may log and review prompts and outputs.

Need a new tool? Ticket. Do not quietly buy a seat on a personal card and "SSO it later."

If this page and [AI policy](/how-to/ai-policy) disagree, follow the stricter rule and tell IT.

Training is required before access. Non-compliance can mean lost access or worse. Illegal use is a legal problem, not an IT ticket.
