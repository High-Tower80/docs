## Purpose

Say how people may use generative AI (including ChatGPT) without leaking data or shipping bad code and architecture advice.

## Scope

Employees, contractors, consultants, and anyone else on Intertrend systems or Intertrend data.

## Rules

- Get manager **and** IT approval before using a gen-AI tool
- Opt out of vendor training / data sharing where the product allows it
- **Never** put in financials, PII, trade secrets, passwords, API keys, or anything that would let an outsider into Intertrend systems
- Give the tool the minimum needed
- Treat output as a draft. Review code, “best practice” designs, and any security, legal, or safety advice
- Do not forward raw output outside the company or to people who should not see it
- Delete chat logs you no longer need
- Business use on company devices only — not personal projects on corporate gear
- Use a tool only for data types it is actually approved to handle

IT may log and review prompts and outputs for compliance.

Training on this policy is required before access. Non-compliance can mean discipline up to termination; illegal use can mean prosecution.

The 2026 staff page ([AI policy](/how-to/ai-policy)) names current approved tools and the ethics rules for client creative. If the two disagree, follow the stricter rule and tell IT so we can reconcile at the next annual review.
