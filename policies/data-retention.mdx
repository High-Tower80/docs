## 1. Purpose

This policy sets how long Intertrend Communications keeps client and business data, how that data is stored and protected, and how it is made available to clients and auditors, so that we meet client agreements and industry standards.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, systems, and vendors that create, store, or process client data, including media placement records, campaign reports, and system logs.

## 3. Definitions

- **Transaction data:** media placement records such as bid prices, budgets, ad-serving data, and engagement metrics.
- **Reporting data:** campaign performance reports and summaries delivered to or prepared for clients.
- **Retention period:** the minimum time data must be kept, as set by this policy or a client agreement.

## 4. Retention periods

| Data type | Minimum retention |
| --- | --- |
| Transaction data | 24 months |
| Reporting data | 12 months |

Where a client agreement or law requires a longer period, the longer period applies. When a retention period ends and no other obligation applies, data is securely deleted.

## 5. Data separation

Each client's data is kept logically separate from other clients' and advertisers' data, through separate shared drives, folders, or accounts with access limited to the people working on that client.

## 6. Availability

- Clients and their authorized auditors are given timely access to records they are entitled to under their agreement.
- Data is provided in formats compatible with the client's systems, such as CSV or spreadsheet exports.
- Company data in Google Workspace is backed up nightly to Backblaze, as described in the [Backup and Recovery Policy](/policies/backup-recovery).

## 7. Security

- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
- Access is limited to authorized roles, and access activity is logged and monitored.

## 8. Audit readiness

- Records and systems are kept in a state that can be audited at any time.
- A self-assessment of retention and availability practices is performed semiannually, alongside the IT risk register review.

## 9. Roles and responsibilities

- **IT Manager:** maintains secure storage, encryption, access controls, and backups, and leads the self-assessment.
- **Operations Director:** confirms retention requirements in client agreements and keeps this policy aligned with them.
- **Project teams:** keep accurate records for the required periods and store them only in approved locations.
- **Vendors:** provide access to records when required and maintain equivalent retention safeguards.

## 10. Enforcement

Violations may result in disciplinary action and may expose Intertrend to contract penalties or financial liability.

## 11. Review and references

This policy is reviewed at least annually and when client agreements or industry standards change.

- ISO/IEC 27001 Information Security Standard
- SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria
- Client master services agreements

<Tab title="Staff quick reference">

## How long we keep client data

- Media placement and transaction data: at least **24 months**
- Campaign reports: at least **12 months**
- If a client contract says longer, follow the contract.

## Where it goes

- Store client files only in Intertrend Google Workspace, in that client's shared drive or folder.
- Keep each client's data separate from other clients' data.
- Never keep client data on personal accounts, USB drives, or personal cloud storage.

## Keeping it safe

- Share client folders only with the people working on that client.
- If a client or auditor asks for records, tell your project lead and IT so we can respond quickly.

## Not sure?

Ask IT before deleting client data or moving it somewhere else. [Submit a help ticket](/how-to/submit-a-help-ticket).
