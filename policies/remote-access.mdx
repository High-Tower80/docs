## Purpose

Let people work off-network without opening the company to drive-by access.

## Scope

Everyone and every vendor who reaches Intertrend systems from outside the office network.

## Rules that apply to you

- **MFA** is required for remote access
- Traffic is encrypted (TLS 1.3 or IPSec / NIST-approved)
- Idle sessions drop (about 10 minutes on this standard; VPN procedures also cite 15 — treat idle as short)
- Least privilege. No dual/split tunnel: all traffic goes through the VPN for inspection
- Only Intertrend-issued or IT-approved devices
- Do not run scanners, sniffers, or denial-of-service tools on the network
- USB and other removable media are restricted and logged
- Sensitive mail leaving the company must be encrypted

Need VPN? Ask IT (purpose, how long, which systems) with your manager’s OK. See [Submit a help ticket](/how-to/submit-a-help-ticket).

Vendors get temporary, watched access only.

## Wireless

WPA3 is the minimum. Guest devices stay on guest Wi-Fi — not the staff LAN. Passwords stay in the wiki, not here.

## Data handling

Classify data as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. DLP watches movement. No unencrypted confidential email outbound.
