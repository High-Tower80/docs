## Purpose

People work off the office LAN. That does not mean the company is open to whatever device happens to have a password.

Identity is the first control. See [Identity and Entra](/policies/identity-entra). This page is the network side: VPN, gear, and session rules.

## Scope

Everyone and every vendor who reaches Intertrend systems from outside the Long Beach or Plano staff LAN.

## Rules that apply to you

- **MFA** on the identity, then the VPN. SMS is not an acceptable factor. Authenticator with number matching or a passkey.
- Traffic is encrypted (TLS 1.3 or IPSec).
- **Idle timeout is 15 minutes.** The old docs disagreed (10 vs 15). 15 is the rule. Do not leave a VPN session up overnight.
- Least privilege. No split tunnel. Inspection happens on the company path.
- Intertrend-issued or IT-approved devices only. Personal laptops do not get staff VPN.
- Do not run scanners, sniffers, or denial-of-service tools.
- USB and other removable media are restricted.
- Confidential mail leaving the company is encrypted.

Need VPN? Ticket with purpose, duration, and systems, plus your manager. [Submit a help ticket](/how-to/submit-a-help-ticket).

Vendors get time-bounded, watched access. No standing vendor VPN because it is convenient.

## Remote support

Prefer **Jamf Remote Assist** on enrolled Macs (user has to accept screen share). ConnectWise / ScreenConnect stays for machines that are not enrolled. Unenrolled remote support is a smell — enroll the device or do not put company data on it.

## Wireless

WPA3 is the minimum. Guests stay on guest Wi-Fi. Passwords stay in the wiki, not here.

## Approved infrastructure (current)

| Role | What we run | Who | RTO |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Firewall / DNS filter | FortiGate 60F | IT | 4 hours |
| Switching | Meraki MS210-48LP | IT | 4 hours |
| Internet | Frontier gigabit fiber | IT | 4 hours |
| Wi-Fi | UniFi U7 Pro | IT | 4 hours |
| Remote access | FortiGate VPN client | IT | Immediate |
| Cloud files | Google Drive | IT | 1 hour |
| Endpoints | Jamf Protect | IT | Continuous |
| SOC / vuln | KMicro managed detection, scanning, and IR retainers | KMicro \+ IT | Per incident |

A single FortiGate 60F is not high availability. If that box dies, remote access and DNS filtering die with it. That is an accepted risk until we buy a pair or a cloud fallback. Do not write "resilient" on a client questionnaire unless you can point at the second path.

Changes to this list need IT approval.

## Data handling

Public / Internal / Confidential / Restricted. DLP watches movement. No unencrypted confidential email outbound.
