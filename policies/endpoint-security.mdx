## Purpose

Secure every company-managed Apple endpoint against malware, unauthorized apps, and data loss.

## Scope

- Company Macs (laptops and desktops)
- Company iPhone and iPad
- Any future company-supported Apple device that joins Intertrend network or cloud services

## Standard

**Jamf Protect** is the endpoint platform. **Jamf Pro** is required MDM. Devices not enrolled do not get company resources.

Do not disable, uninstall, or tamper with Jamf Protect. Keep OS and Jamf policies current.

## Requirements

**Protection**

- Jamf Protect monitors for malware, ransomware, and suspicious behavior
- Only apps from the Intertrend IT App Store
- FileVault on, firewall on
- USB and external drives restricted to limit exfiltration
- Compliance checked before access (zero trust)

**Network**

- Remote access uses Intertrend VPN (Google Workspace identity)
- Office Wi-Fi is for managed, compliant devices only
- Jamf Protect DNS filtering blocks known-bad sites

**Mobile**

- No jailbroken or rooted devices
- Face ID or Touch ID required for corporate access
- Report lost or stolen devices to IT immediately — remote wipe is enabled

**If something looks wrong**

Jamf can quarantine a device and isolate it from the network. Events go to Intertrend’s SIEM. Report odd behavior via [Submit a help ticket](/how-to/submit-a-help-ticket).

## Your job

Stay enrolled. Do not install random software. Follow password, phishing, and acceptable-use rules.

Non-compliance can mean revoked access, HR review, or the device being turned off.

IT audits quarterly. This policy is reviewed annually.
