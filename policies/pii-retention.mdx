## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how Intertrend Communications collects, retains, protects, and disposes of personally identifiable information (PII), in line with client requirements and privacy laws.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, systems, and vendors that handle PII on behalf of Intertrend or its clients, including PII about consumers, client contacts, and employees.

## 3. Definitions

- **PII:** information that can identify a person, such as a name, email address, phone number, postal address, or device identifier.
- **Privacy Administrator:** the person responsible for PII governance and the point of contact for privacy questions and incidents.

## 4. Governance

The IT Manager serves as Intertrend's Privacy Administrator unless leadership designates another person. The Privacy Administrator is the point of contact for all PII matters, for staff and for clients.

## 5. Collection and use

- PII is collected only when it is needed to deliver a service or meet a legal or contractual obligation.
- Only the minimum PII needed is collected and shared.
- PII is anonymized or pseudonymized wherever the work allows it.

## 6. Retention and disposal

- PII is kept only as long as a client agreement, law, or business need requires.
- When that period ends, PII is securely deleted. Digital records are deleted from active systems and allowed to age out of backups; paper records are shredded.
- Devices that held PII are wiped before reuse or disposal.

## 7. Security safeguards

- PII is encrypted in transit and at rest.
- Access to PII is role-based, limited to people who need it, and logged.
- PII is not entered into AI tools except as permitted by the [AI Use Policy](/policies/generative-ai).
- Vendors that handle PII must provide equivalent safeguards under the [Third Party Risk Management Policy](/policies/third-party-risk).

## 8. Incidents

Any suspected loss, exposure, or misuse of PII is reported immediately to the Privacy Administrator and handled under the [Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan](/policies/incident-response), including any notice required to clients, individuals, or regulators.

## 9. Roles and responsibilities

- **Privacy Administrator:** oversees compliance with this policy, manages PII incidents, and reports to leadership.
- **IT Manager:** implements encryption, access controls, and monitoring.
- **Operations Director:** confirms PII requirements in client agreements and supports staff training.
- **All staff:** handle PII carefully and report concerns or incidents promptly.
- **Vendors:** demonstrate that PII they handle is protected.

## 10. Enforcement

Violations may result in disciplinary action, termination of vendor agreements, and legal or regulatory consequences.

## 11. Review and references

This policy is reviewed at least annually and when privacy laws or client agreements change.

- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA)
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), where applicable
- ISO/IEC 27701 Privacy Information Management

## What counts as PII

- Names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, and device IDs that point to a real person
- This includes consumers in client data, client contacts, and coworkers.

## Do

- Collect and share only the PII the work actually needs.
- Store it only in approved Intertrend systems with access limited to people who need it.
- Delete it when the project or contract no longer requires it.

## Don't

- Put PII in AI tools unless the [AI Use Policy](/policies/generative-ai) allows it.
- Email spreadsheets of PII outside Intertrend or save them to personal accounts or USB drives.

## If something goes wrong

Report any lost, exposed, or misused PII to IT immediately: `/assist` in Slack or [submit a help ticket](/how-to/submit-a-help-ticket). The Privacy Administrator is Tate Allen, IT Manager.
