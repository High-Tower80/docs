## Purpose

Detect, contain, and recover from unauthorized access, malware, phishing, DDoS, and insider issues. Align with CCPA, GDPR, and NIST CSF.

## Who is on the IRT

| Role | Who | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incident Response Manager | IT Security Lead | Runs the response |
| Data Protection Officer | COO | CCPA / GDPR |
| IT Security Analysts | IT | Forensics and containment |
| Legal | Counsel | Notice language |
| Communications | Managing Director | Internal and public messaging |
| Cloud and infrastructure | IT | GCP restore |
| Vendors | Backblaze, Google Cloud Security, and others as needed | Forensics and mitigation |

## Phases

**0–1 hour — detect**<br />SIEM / EDR alert → confirm impact → isolate endpoints, block bad IPs, revoke creds → open a ticket with a case ID.

**1–6 hours — investigate**<br />GCP Security and Microsoft Defender. Find the path in. Check whether PII, financials, or credentials leaked.

**6–12 hours — contain**<br />Patch, restore from Backblaze or GCP, MFA reset, phishing warning if needed.

**12–24 hours — notify**<br />Leadership and affected staff. If **500\+ customer records**, CCPA / California AG. Customers via email, SMS, or a secure portal. Law enforcement if it looks criminal.

**24–48 hours — recover**<br />Bring services back. Watch for leftovers. Tighten controls.

**48–72 hours — review**<br />Post-mortem, training, playbook update.

## Cloud backup posture

No dedicated DR site. **GCP \+ Backblaze B2**. Endpoints: Jamf Protect and Defender. DNS filtering is on the FortiGate (Cisco Umbrella appears in the 2024 plan as a capability; live gear is listed under [Remote access](/policies/remote-access)).

Log everything in the SIEM. Annual tabletop. Records stay in encrypted cloud storage.

Staff: if you think you clicked something bad, do not wait for the 72-hour clock. [Report phishing](/how-to/phishing-alert-guide) or `/assist` now.
