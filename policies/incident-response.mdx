This is the **approved plan**. Short IT checklist: [Incident response SOP](/sops/incident-response) and [Phishing triage](/sops/phishing-triage).

## Purpose

Detect, contain, and recover from account takeover, malware, phishing, denial of service, and insider issues. Align with CCPA, other privacy law that actually applies, and NIST CSF.

The 2024 plan talked about SIEM, Umbrella, and a clean 72-hour clock. The incident we actually had was a mailbox that mailed the outside world for hours after two months of quiet abuse. Write the plan for that, not for a textbook ransomware cartoon.

## What we do first (account takeover)

This is the pattern we already lived:

1. Disable the account or revoke sessions and refresh tokens.
2. Reset credentials. Remove and re-register MFA. Kill SMS if it is still there.
3. Search for inbox rules, forwarding, delegates, Send-as, and OAuth / enterprise apps.
4. Pull Entra sign-in logs and, if licensed, Identity Protection and Graph activity logs.
5. Check the endpoint in Jamf Protect. Isolate if needed.
6. Block known-bad domains and IPs.
7. Decide who outside the company must be told. Unauthorized mail to third parties is an external incident, not "just IT."
8. Write the timeline in a private Drive folder. Names and recipient lists stay off this site and off public Slack.

## Phases (clock starts when IT or KMicro confirms, not when someone "has a bad feeling")

**0–1 hour — detect and isolate.** Confirm impact. Isolate endpoints. Revoke creds. Open a ticket with a case ID. Call KMicro if identity or mail is involved.

**1–6 hours — investigate.** Entra, Microsoft 365, Google admin audit, Jamf. Find the path in. Check PII, financials, credentials, mailbox rules, OAuth.

**6–12 hours — contain.** Patch or reimage. Restore if needed. MFA reset at scale if the factor is burned. Staff warning if phishing is still landing.

**12–24 hours — notify.** Leadership and affected staff. If **500\+ consumer records**, California AG / CCPA analysis with Legal. Customers by a channel Legal approves. Law enforcement if it looks criminal.

**24–48 hours — recover.** Bring services back. Watch for leftovers.

**48–72 hours — review.** Post-mortem with gaps, not compliments. Update this page if the plan was wrong.

## Logging we must have

June 2026: tenant on Entra ID Free, no Identity Protection, no Graph activity logging. KMicro could not reconstruct Graph API actions or prove initial access. Containment still worked. Attribution did not.

**24 August 2026:** still no P1 or P2. Business Standard only. Identity Protection policies cannot be enabled. Conditional Access cannot be created. The logging gap did not close itself.

Required after that incident:

- Entra P2 or equivalent, Identity Protection on
- Graph activity logging on and retained
- SOC able to see identity alerts
- Jamf Protect telemetry to the SOC (discussed March 2026, **not confirmed live**)

If those are still off, say so in the post-mortem. Do not write "full visibility."

## Cloud backup posture

No dedicated DR site. **Workspace \+ Backblaze B2** and **KMicro server backups**. Endpoints: Jamf Protect only. DNS filtering is the FortiGate. Cisco Umbrella appears in the 2024 PDF as a capability; do not list it unless it is still paid and on.

Annual tabletop is required. Last tabletop date is **not on this site**.
