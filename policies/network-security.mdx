## Purpose

Keep the office network from being the easy door, and keep mail from being the easy door. Mail already was.

## What you must not do

- Sniff, scan, or attack the network
- Attach unapproved gear (including a random access point or "just a travel router")
- Share your login
- Send confidential or cardholder data in clear email
- Use FTP, Telnet, or other clear-text admin protocols

## How access works

- Internet-bound traffic goes through the FortiGate (URL categories, attachment scan).
- Remote access is documented, logged, and MFA-backed. See [Remote access](/policies/remote-access) and [Identity and Entra](/policies/identity-entra).
- VPN: AES-256, IKEv2/IPSec, no split tunnel, IT-approved devices only.
- Guest and BYOD stay on isolated VLANs with internet only.
- Vendor tools and customer-network links need IT plus the data owner.

## Email authentication (this is not optional)

June 2026 was a mailbox compromise that mailed ~297 outsiders from a real Intertrend account. Perimeter filtering does not catch that.

| Control | Required |
| --- | --- |
| SPF | Published for every sending domain |
| DKIM | On |
| DMARC | **p=reject** (or quarantine with a dated plan to reject). Monitoring-only is not a control |
| External banners | On for mail from outside |
| Forwarding / inbox rules | IT reviews after incidents and on offboarding |

As of June 2026, DMARC was moved from monitoring **toward** enforcement. **Reject-on-all-domains is not confirmed.** Until it is, say "in progress" on questionnaires, not "enforced."

KMicro runs external / web application testing and vulnerability management on a retainer. Treat their findings as tickets, not PDFs that die in Drive.

## Data loss prevention

| Class | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Public | Fine to share widely |
| Internal | Staff only |
| Confidential | Named people / need to know |
| Restricted | Encryption and IT controls |

DLP watches email, Drive, and endpoints where it is actually turned on. USB is restricted. Do not claim Vault / DLP coverage that only exists in a Business Plus pitch deck.

## Wireless and guests

WPA3 minimum (WPA2 only with extra controls). Staff use managed APs. Guests use guest Wi-Fi. Do not put visitors on the staff LAN. SSID and passwords stay in the wiki.

Personal laptops: manager plus IT before any staff LAN. Internet-only goes on guest. VPN is not for non-standard personal machines.

## Incidents

Odd network behavior is an incident. See [Incident response](/policies/incident-response) and the [IT SOP](/sops/incident-response).

Violations can be a disciplinary matter. Exceptions go through IT Exceptions, not Slack.
