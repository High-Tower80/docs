## Purpose

Keep the Intertrend network reliable and keep data from walking out the side door.

## What you must not do

- Sniff, scan, or attack the network
- Attach unapproved gear (including random Wi-Fi access points or modems)
- Share your login
- Send confidential or cardholder data in unencrypted email
- Use FTP, Telnet, or other clear-text admin protocols

## How access works

- Internet-bound traffic goes through filtering (URL categories, email attachment scan, SPF/DKIM/DMARC)
- Remote access is documented, logged, and MFA-backed. See [Remote access](/policies/remote-access)
- VPN uses AES-256 and IKEv2/IPSec, no split tunnel, IT-approved devices only
- Guest and BYOD stay on isolated VLANs with internet only
- Vendor tools and customer-network links need the IT Manager plus the data owner

## Data loss prevention

| Class | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Public | Fine to share widely |
| Internal | Staff only |
| Confidential | Named people / need to know |
| Restricted | Highest care; encryption and IT controls |

DLP watches email, Drive, and endpoints. USB is restricted. Alerts go to security.

## Wireless and guests

WPA3 is the minimum (WPA2 only with extra controls). Staff use managed access points. Guests use guest Wi-Fi. Do not put visitors on the staff LAN. SSID and passwords stay in the wiki.

Personal laptops need manager email plus IT before any staff LAN. Internet-only use goes on the guest network. VPN is not for non-standard personal machines.

## Incidents

Treat odd network behavior as an incident. See [Incident response](/policies/incident-response) and the [IT SOP](/sops/incident-response).

Violations can be a disciplinary matter. Exceptions go through the IT Exceptions process, not Slack.
