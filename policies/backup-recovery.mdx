## Purpose

A delete, outage, or ransomware event is recoverable. A number on a slide is not a restore.

## What is backed up

- **Google Workspace** (Backblaze B2): Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, Contacts, Shared Drives
- **Headquarters servers** (KMicro managed backup), including domain, certificate services, RDS, and SQL. Host names stay in the MSP runbook, not in Slack
- Cloud instances, configs, and logs that operations actually need

If a SaaS app is not in this list, assume it is **not** backed up unless IT said so in a ticket.

## Cadence

| Data | How often | Keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Workspace to Backblaze B2 | Daily, plus incremental every 4 hours for critical files | Drive versions 90 days; Gmail / Calendar / Contacts 1 year |
| HQ servers (KMicro) | Daily; monthly full encrypted offsite | Annual archive for compliance |

Encrypted in transit and at rest (AES-256). MFA on backup consoles. Unauthorized delete blocked where the platform allows it. Backup admin is IT and KMicro only.

## Restore targets

- **RPO:** no more than 4 hours of data loss
- **RTO:** critical data back within 1 hour

Those targets are the standard. **Last successful restore drill is not dated on this site.** Until IT files a dated Workspace restore and a dated server restore, treat the RTO as an objective, not a tested fact. Do not tell a carrier or a client we "test quarterly" unless you can point at the last ticket.

**How a restore goes:** assess what is missing → Backblaze for Workspace or KMicro for servers → restore to original or alternate location → check integrity → tell affected users → review logs.

Priority in a major event: executive mail, active Drive jobs, financial systems, customer contracts and compliance files.

Need a file back? [Submit a help ticket](/how-to/submit-a-help-ticket). Do not ask in a public channel with client names.

## Open gaps

1. File the last restore test (date, what was restored, how long it took, who signed off).
2. Confirm Backblaze still covers every Shared Drive that matters. Shared Drive sprawl is how we miss a job.
3. Confirm MFA and immutable / object-lock settings on B2. Ransomware that can delete backups is not a hypothetical.
4. SaaS apps outside Workspace (Notion, Figma, Hub, etc.) need an explicit "backed up" or "accepted loss" decision. Silence is accepted loss.
