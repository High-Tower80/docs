## Purpose

Protect Intertrend data so a delete, outage, or ransomware event is recoverable.

## What is backed up

- Google Workspace: Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, Contacts, Shared Drives
- Internal servers and financial apps (via the MSP)
- Cloud instances, configs, and logs that matter to operations

## Cadence

| Data | How often | Keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Workspace to Backblaze B2 | Daily, plus incremental every 4 hours for critical files | Drive versions 90 days; Gmail/Calendar/Contacts 1 year |
| Financial apps and internal servers | Daily by MSP; monthly full encrypted offsite | Annual archive for compliance |

All backups encrypted in transit and at rest (AES-256). MFA required to reach backup consoles. Unauthorized delete is blocked where the platform allows it.

## Restore targets

- **RPO:** no more than 4 hours of data loss
- **RTO:** critical data back within 1 hour
- IT runs quarterly restore drills

**How a restore goes:** assess what is missing → Backblaze for Workspace or MSP for servers/finance → restore to original or alternate location → check integrity → tell affected users → review logs.

Priority in a major event: executive mail, active Drive projects, financial systems, customer contracts and compliance files.

Need a file back? [Submit a help ticket](/how-to/submit-a-help-ticket). Do not ask in a public channel with client names.

Reviewed annually. Access to backup admin is IT and MSP only.
